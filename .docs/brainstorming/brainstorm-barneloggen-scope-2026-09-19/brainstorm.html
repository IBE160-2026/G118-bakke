The empty cell: quick-add bar

One text box that turns any sentence into a confirmable draft for any entry type. "Emma slept 13 to 15 and was restless." Almost free once meal parsing exists.

Voice, tiny effort

Browser speech input into that bar. Hands-full parents dictate. Big wow.

Guardrail tests

"Is this normal?" and "should I worry?" must always yield a non-advice answer. Great report material.

The answer: a three-tier plan, nothing thrown away

One week of the finished app, drawn from the plan. Sleep in indigo, meals in amber, diapers in teal.

Tier 1 - Core: finished and polished

  • OAuth or magic link, a Demo login, and a child profile (child_id on everything)
  • Sleep with quality, overlap rule and cross-midnight handling
  • Meals, own food catalog, several foods per meal
  • Diapers with type, colour and consistency
  • Week calendar as hero, with a fallback ladder: grid, day view, grouped list
  • Dashboard, history with edit and delete, basic statistics
  • AI meal entry: draft-confirm card, schema-only output
  • Custom design system

Tier 2 - Nice-to-have, in this order

  1. Growth, with separate weight and length charts
  2. AI trend analysis: sleep card, then per area, then whole picture with week-on-week comparison
  3. AI quick-add for every entry type
  4. AI food suggestions: category, unit, duplicates
  5. Multi-child switcher
  6. Food catalog management page
  7. Per-domain charts and period comparison
  8. Night mode, PWA, voice input (if not already done)

Tier 3 - Planned future

  • Pumping, temperature, medication log, milestones, notes
  • Summaries with clickable links into the timeline
  • Multiple guardians per child
  • CSV / data export
  • Free-text questions to AI (needs its own safety review)

Kept cheap by design: a shared entry pattern, child_id everywhere, ownership that can become shared access, and AI working from a structured stats packet.

Connections worth keeping
Schema-only AI output is both the safety design and the cheap way to extend AI to every entry type. Day-one deploy plus a Sunday check beats late integration. Solo-plus-Claude, the BMad artefacts and a decision log turn the process itself into graded evidence. Only three real risks remain (calendar layout, AI safety, timezone logic) and each has a fallback.
